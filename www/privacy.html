Privacy Policy — GharBijli

GharBijli ("we", "our", or "us") provides on-demand doorstep electrical repair and installation services across Bhilai and Durg (Chhattisgarh, India). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our customers and partner electricians.

1. Information We Collect

When you book an electrical service or use our app, we collect minimal information necessary to fulfill your doorstep service request:

  • Contact Information: Full name and mobile phone number (used to confirm bookings and coordinate arrival).
  • Location & Address: House number, street, sector, and landmark in Bhilai/Durg (used by the assigned electrician to travel to your doorstep).
  • Service Details: Electrical issues reported, appliances involved, and preferred inspection time slot.
  • Authentication: Google profile identifier (name, email) if signing in with Google One-Tap.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To assign and dispatch verified electrical partners in Bhilai.
  • To send real-time service status updates and dispatch notifications.
  • To provide Cash on Delivery (COD) invoices and service receipts.
  • To handle emergency electrical breakdown requests (15-Minute SLA).

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing agencies. Your address and contact phone are shared solely with the assigned GharBijli electrical partner for the active service visit.

All data is stored securely in modern encrypted cloud infrastructure (MongoDB Atlas) with access control and authentication barriers.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD) Payment Privacy

We do not store or process debit card numbers, credit card numbers, or bank passwords inside our application. All payments are completed in cash directly after the electrical work is verified and tested at your doorstep.
